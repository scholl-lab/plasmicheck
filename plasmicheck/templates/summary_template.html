How to Interpret Boundary Mismatches

Counts reads within ±100 bp of the cDNA cloning boundaries that show mismatches (NM > 0) or soft/hard clipping (S/H) on the plasmid reference:

Native Genomic Reads (Clean): Intron/UTR tails diverge at the cloning site, causing soft-clipping without vector backbone coverage.
Plasmid Reads (Contaminated): Reads span smoothly into the vector backbone (VI junctions) alongside elevated backbone breadth.
