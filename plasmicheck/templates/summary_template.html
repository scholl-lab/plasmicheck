Boundary Mismatch & Clipping Interpretation

Counts reads overlapping the cDNA insert boundaries (±100 bp) that exhibit sequence mismatches (NM > 0) or soft/hard clipping (S/H CIGAR operations) when aligned to the plasmid reference.

Native Genomic Reads (Clean Sample)
Reads spanning exon–intron or UTR boundaries diverge from the plasmid vector cloning site, producing soft-clipped tails and elevated boundary mismatch counts relative to clean insert interiors.
Plasmid-Derived Reads (Contaminated Sample)
Reads crossing the vector–insert junction align contiguously into the plasmid backbone without clipping, while high total read depth at the insert boundary can also elevate raw boundary counts alongside high backbone coverage.

Evaluate alongside Coverage Outside Insert and Backbone Breadth to distinguish true vector-junction reads from native genomic boundary clipping.
